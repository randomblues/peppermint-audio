# Google Ads campaign context

This document records the campaign setup and decisions reviewed on 13 October
2026. Google Ads status and performance change over time; recheck the live
account before making further changes.

## Goal and guardrails

- Prioritise qualified Melbourne audio-equipment hire enquiries, not clicks or
  general website traffic.
- Keep targeting aligned with the actual PA, speaker, microphone, and event
  packages offered by Peppermint Audio. Equipment is collected and returned in
  Abbotsford.
- Use phrase-match keywords. Do not switch to broad match or apply
  Google-recommended changes automatically; assess their likely value against
  lead quality and the campaign's limited budget.
- Keep relevant "near me" phrases in the product or occasion group they match.
  They are not a separate theme by themselves.
- Judge changes by qualified booking requests and their outcomes, not CTR or
  clicks alone.

## Campaign snapshot

At the last review, the campaign was **Peppermint Audio Local Search**, a Search
campaign with a **$10/day budget**, **Maximize Clicks** bidding, and a **15 km
radius around Abbotsford**. The budget and bidding strategy were not changed
during the ad and image updates below.

There were 75 active keywords, all phrase match, with no broad-match keywords:

| Ad group | Theme | Phrase keywords |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| PA, Speaker & Sound System Hire | PA and speaker equipment hire | 43 |
| Party, Wedding & Event Audio | Occasion-based audio and packages | 23 |

The list includes 23 relevant "near me" phrases. At review, 74 keywords were

checking its intent and future value.

## Ad groups and ads

Each group has its own tailored responsive search ad (RSA), so the copy and
landing page match the search theme:

- **PA, Speaker & Sound System Hire** → `https://www.peppermintaudio.com.au/equipment`
- **Party, Wedding & Event Audio** → `https://www.peppermintaudio.com.au/packages`
- **Microphones & Speech Audio** → `https://www.peppermintaudio.com.au/equipment`

Each RSA was saved with 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. The copy focuses on
the relevant hire need, Abbotsford collection, and submitting a booking
request; a booking request is not a confirmed hire. The previous generic and
corny slogans, including "Rock your party and impress your friends," were
removed. At the last check, all three ads were Eligible, with ad strength
Pending and no policy issues reported.

The groups share the campaign budget and bidding strategy. Keep the separation
only while each theme has meaningfully relevant keywords, ad copy, and landing
page; do not split "near me" into its own group without a distinct customer
intent.

## Conversion measurement

A website booking-submission conversion was added earlier. Click-only
conversions were set to **Secondary** so they do not guide bidding. Verify
that the booking conversion fires for a real test submission before relying on
conversion reports to assess lead quality. A "Misconfigured" diagnostic is a
separate issue; marking a click conversion Secondary does not resolve a broken
tag or conversion setup.
